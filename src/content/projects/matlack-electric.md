## What I do

Full-service residential electrical for renovations and new builds, plus light commercial work. One contractor handles electrical, low-voltage, and controls together, so clients don't have to coordinate several trades.

- **Custom lighting.** Lighting design and installation, done in-house.
- **Lutron controls and shades.** Certified on Caséta and RadioRA 3.
- **EV charging.** Tesla Certified Installer, including load management.
- **Backup power.** Generators, battery backup, and load management.
- **Low-voltage and networking.** Structured wiring, whole-home WiFi, and A/V.

## The business

I started Matlack Electric in Pennsylvania in 2020, while I was still in school. It grew from a small service-call operation into a full-service contractor, and in 2026 I moved it to Chattanooga. However it grows, I'm still the one on site, from the first call through the final walkthrough.

In 2021, after Hurricane Ida, I volunteered to restore power to about 30 households in my area, work the local paper covered.
